What makes SkedioAI feel different

One system for planning, progress, and calm course-correction.

It is not just a timetable generator. SkedioAI is meant to carry the full loop: intake, feasibility, scheduling, progress truth, and future repair when life gets messy.

01

Natural planning intake

Students talk normally. Saathi gathers the real goal, scope, constraints, and time window without turning the experience into a form.

02

Grounded weekly plans

Plans are built around workload truth, not fake optimism. The system checks what fits, what does not, and what needs repair.

03

Calendar-aware scheduling

External blockers and timing boundaries help prevent impossible plans before they land in the student’s week.

04

Checkboxes with real feedback

Session completion, skips, and actual hours are tracked so progress becomes durable truth instead of wishful memory.

05

Rescheduling that preserves trust

When time is missed or the week changes, Saathi repairs the future instead of dumping chaos back on the student.

06

Progress-aware memory

Backlog truth, study history, and weak areas help later plans feel personal instead of generic.
